# P1 — Stage 07 Human Evaluation Evidence

## Record Status

- **Stage:** Stage 07 — Validation
- **Evidence type:** First human evaluation of the prototype
- **Evaluator:** Project owner / engineer
- **Status:** Consolidated evidence record
- **Scope:** Human evaluation of the accepted Stage 06 prototype
- **Stage 07 acceptance:** Not yet established

## 1. Overall UI

The evaluator experienced the prototype and judged the UI to be **okay**.

**Finding:** The general UI was acceptable to the first human evaluator.

## 2. Project 01 Understandability

The evaluator found it clear:

- what Project 01 was; and
- what they were expected to understand from the Project 01 screen before entering deeper technical material.

**Finding:** Established through first-person human evaluation.

## 3. Progressive Depth

The progression from:

**Project 01 → Engineering Approach / Engineering Record → Deep Engineering**

felt natural and understandable.

The evaluator understood why the deeper areas existed and how they related to Project 01.

**Finding:** Established through first-person human evaluation.

## 4. Navigation

The navigation felt **absolutely okay**.

The evaluator experienced the navigation as clear and predictable, without confusion from competing or unnecessary navigation options.

Return paths also felt **natural**.

**Finding:** Established through first-person human evaluation.

## 5. Engineering Approach and Engineering Record

The distinction between **Engineering Approach** and **Engineering Record** was clear to the evaluator.

The evaluator understood why the two areas existed and what different information would be expected in each.

**Finding:** Established through first-person human evaluation.

## 6. Deep Engineering

Deep Engineering felt like a deliberate deeper level of the same engineering experience.

The evaluator understood why they were being taken deeper and what they were expected to inspect there.

**Finding:** Established through first-person human evaluation.

## 7. Evidence Discoverability

The evaluator did **not** feel that the prototype gave enough indication of where the actual engineering evidence lived and how it could be inspected.

**Finding:** Evidence discoverability is currently insufficient from the first evaluator's experience.

## 8. Evidence Sufficiency

Once reaching the evidence-related areas, the evaluator considered the available basis for evaluating the engineering work **partially sufficient**.

**Finding:** Some basis for evaluation exists, but the evidence is not yet sufficient for full evaluation.

## 9. Engineering Journey and Written Content

The prototype currently shows the **resulting structure**, but does not make the actual engineering journey or the written content clear.

**Finding:** The structural experience is present, but the engineering journey is not yet adequately portrayed through content.

## 10. Decision-Making Evidence

The evaluator could not clearly tell:

- why important engineering decisions were made;
- what problem or uncertainty led to a decision; or
- what changed as a result.

**Finding:** Decision-making evidence is not yet clear through the prototype.

## 11. Revisions and Learning

The evaluator could not clearly tell:

- what was revised;
- what was learned from those revisions; or
- how the engineering direction changed over time.

**Finding:** Revision and learning evidence is not yet clear through the prototype.

## 12. Remaining Unresolved Matter

The remaining unresolved matter is **the content needed to support the existing structure and portray the actual engineering journey**, so that the evidence can be understood as a **coherent system**.

This indicates that the principal unresolved issue identified through this first human evaluation is currently **evidence/content materialization**, rather than a fundamental failure of the prototype's navigation or structural UI.

## 13. Boundary

This evaluation does **not** authorize:

- redesigning the prototype;
- restructuring the accepted Stage 06 experience;
- implementing frontend functionality;
- creating production code;
- changing Penpot;
- or beginning Stage 08 implementation.

The findings are evidence for determining what should happen next. Any response to the findings requires a separate human decision and authorization.

## Current Validation Disposition

**Validated aspects:**
- General UI
- Project 01 understandability
- Progressive depth
- Navigation
- Approach / Record distinction
- Deep Engineering purpose
- Return paths

**Partially validated:**
- Sufficiency of engineering evidence

**Not yet sufficiently established:**
- Evidence discoverability
- Engineering journey through written content
- Decision-making traceability
- Revision and learning traceability

**Primary remaining gap:**

> **Content and evidence materialization needed to support the existing structure and portray the actual engineering journey as a coherent system.**

**Stage 07 status:** Active — validation findings established, acceptance pending.
