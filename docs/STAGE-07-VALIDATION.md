# STAGE 07 — VALIDATION

**Stage:** 07 of 10  
**Status:** Active — Authorized for execution within approved boundary  
**Primary Instrument:** Penpot prototype + engineering record  
**Preceding Stage:** Stage 06 — Prototype  
**Following Stage:** Stage 08 — Engineering Implementation  
**Authorization:** Human-approved for execution within this boundary

---

## 1. Purpose

Stage 07 exists to determine whether the accepted Stage 06 prototype provides sufficient evidence that the intended portfolio experience is understandable, traversable, coherent, and ready to proceed toward engineering implementation.

The stage evaluates the prototype as an experience and engineering artifact.

It does not assume that successful interaction wiring means that the experience itself is successful.

---

## 2. Validation Questions

Stage 07 should seek evidence for the following questions:

### Experience

- Can a visitor understand what the portfolio is presenting?
- Can a visitor understand what Project 01 represents without immediately requiring deep technical detail?
- Can the visitor discover the Engineering Approach and Engineering Record naturally?
- Does the progression from Project 01 toward Deep Engineering make sense?
- Does the progressive-depth model remain coherent as the visitor moves deeper?

### Navigation

- Can the intended journeys be followed without confusion?
- Do the available transitions support the intended information hierarchy?
- Are back/return paths understandable?
- Are there unnecessary or competing navigation mechanisms?

### Evidence

- Does the experience provide enough evidence for the visitor to inspect the engineering work?
- Are important decisions and problems represented where appropriate?
- Can revisions and learning be traced?
- Are uncertainties and deferred matters visible rather than silently hidden?

### Boundary

- Does the prototype remain within the accepted Stage 06 boundary?
- Has validation revealed any reason to reconsider an earlier decision?
- Are any required changes actually validation findings rather than unsupported redesign preferences?

---

## 3. Scope

Stage 07 includes:

- Human inspection of the accepted prototype experience.
- Evaluation of the intended portfolio journey.
- Evaluation of progressive information depth.
- Evaluation of navigation clarity.
- Evaluation of evidence discoverability.
- Identification and documentation of problems.
- Recording observations and interpretations separately.
- Recording validation findings.
- Identifying gaps that require further engineering work.
- Determining whether the prototype provides sufficient clarity to proceed toward Stage 08.

---

## 4. Non-Scope

Stage 07 does **not** include:

- Frontend implementation.
- Backend implementation.
- Production deployment.
- Rebuilding the portfolio.
- Creating a speculative component system.
- Introducing new visual foundations without evidence.
- Reopening Stage 05 merely because a preference emerges.
- Treating prototype verification as production verification.
- Full production accessibility testing.
- Full responsive implementation.
- Making architectural decisions required by Stage 08.
- Implementing fixes simply because a validation observation was recorded.

A validation finding is evidence for a decision; it is not automatically authorization to change the product.

---

## 5. Validation Method

The validation process should preserve the project's engineering distinction between:

**Observation → Interpretation → Finding → Proposed Response → Human Decision → Authorization → Change → Verification**

Where possible, validation should record:

- What was observed.
- Where it was observed.
- Why it may matter.
- What interpretation follows.
- What remains uncertain.
- What response is proposed, if any.
- Whether the response is necessary before Stage 08.

Human judgment remains responsible for acceptance.

---

## 6. Expected Evidence

Stage 07 should produce an evidence record containing, where applicable:

- Validation objectives.
- Validation method.
- Observations.
- Problems encountered.
- Positive evidence.
- Interpretation of findings.
- Unresolved questions.
- Proposed changes, if any.
- Human decisions.
- Authorized changes, if any.
- Verification of authorized changes.
- Final Stage 07 disposition.

The evidence record should distinguish findings from decisions and should not convert every observation into a defect.

---

## 7. Acceptance Criteria

Stage 07 may be considered ready for acceptance when there is sufficient evidence to determine:

### Experience

- Whether the intended portfolio journey is understandable.
- Whether Project 01 provides an appropriate entry into the engineering work.
- Whether Engineering Approach and Engineering Record are discoverable and meaningful.
- Whether Deep Engineering represents an intentional increase in depth.
- Whether the overall progressive-depth model is sufficiently coherent.

### Navigation

- Whether the intended navigation supports the experience.
- Whether significant navigation confusion or dead ends remain.
- Whether any required navigation changes have been identified and dispositioned.

### Evidence

- Whether the prototype exposes enough evidence for meaningful inspection.
- Whether important decisions, problems, revisions, uncertainties, and learning are represented sufficiently for this stage.
- Whether significant evidence gaps have been identified and dispositioned.

### Boundary

- Whether validation remained within the authorized Stage 07 scope.
- Whether any proposed changes have been separately authorized.
- Whether the resulting state is sufficiently clear to inform Stage 08.

---

## 8. Possible Outcomes

Stage 07 does not have to produce a simple “pass/fail” result.

The final disposition may establish one of the following:

- **Validated — sufficient clarity to proceed**
- **Validated with bounded revisions required**
- **Insufficient clarity — further validation required**
- **Blocked by unresolved evidence**

The selected disposition must be supported by the recorded evidence.

---

## 9. Authorization Boundary

Stage 07 authorization covers **validation and evidence gathering within the approved scope**.

It does not automatically authorize:

- redesign,
- implementation,
- architecture,
- production development,
- or changes to previously accepted stages.

If validation reveals a material need to change the boundary, the work should stop at that point and the new decision should be presented for human authorization.

---

## 10. Governing Principle

Stage 07 follows the project's central engineering principle:

> **Understand → Push Forward Under Uncertainty → Evaluate → Identify Gaps → Iterate → Establish Sufficient Clarity**

The purpose of validation is therefore not to prove that the prototype is perfect.

It is to establish **sufficient clarity about what has been built, what works, what does not, what remains uncertain, and what should happen next.**
