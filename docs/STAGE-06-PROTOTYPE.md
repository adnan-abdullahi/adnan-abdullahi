# STAGE 06 — PROTOTYPE

**Project:** Project 01 — Professional Engineering Portfolio  
**Stage:** 06 of 10  
**Status:** Active — Authorized for execution within approved boundary  
**Primary Instrument:** Penpot  
**Preceding Stage:** Stage 05 — Portfolio Screens / Portfolio Orientation  
**Following Stage:** Stage 07 — Validation

---

## 0. Stage Boundary

This record establishes the approved execution boundary for Stage 06.

The boundary was derived from the previously established portfolio experience model and the accepted Stage 05 handoff.

The intended experience is:

**Portfolio Orientation → Project 01 → Engineering Approach / Engineering Record → Deep Engineering**

Engineering Approach and Engineering Record are dimensions of the Project 01 experience. Their inclusion does not, by itself, require separate top-level destinations or an Engineering Record index.

---

## 1. Purpose

Stage 06 exists to turn the accepted Portfolio Orientation and established Project 01 experience model into a traversable interactive prototype that allows the intended visitor journey and progressive inspection depth to be experienced and evaluated before implementation.

The purpose is to establish and examine the experience, not to produce the production implementation.

---

## 2. Scope

Stage 06 covers:

### 2.1 Prototype Structure

- Connect the accepted Portfolio Orientation to Project 01.
- Establish the intended navigation and transitions between relevant experiences.
- Preserve the accepted information hierarchy.

### 2.2 Project 01 Experience

- Materialize the Project 01 entry experience.
- Expose the Engineering Approach.
- Expose the Engineering Record.
- Provide a path into Deep Engineering.

### 2.3 Progressive Inspection

- Allow a visitor to move from orientation toward professional evidence and then deeper engineering evidence.
- Ensure the visitor is not forced immediately into technical depth.
- Preserve the established progressive-depth model.

### 2.4 Prototype Interaction

- Create only interactions necessary to experience the intended journey.
- Establish meaningful prototype connections.
- Avoid speculative interactions whose destinations have not been established.

### 2.5 Experience Evaluation

- Inspect the prototype as a visitor.
- Determine whether the intended journey is understandable.
- Identify friction, ambiguity, missing context, or unsupported assumptions.
- Revise where evidence demonstrates a need.

### 2.6 Evidence

- Preserve meaningful prototype decisions.
- Preserve important discovered problems and revisions.
- Maintain traceability between the prototype and established engineering decisions.

---

## 3. Non-Scope

The following are outside Stage 06 unless new evidence and an explicitly authorized boundary change establish otherwise:

- frontend implementation;
- production code;
- backend or system implementation;
- production accessibility testing;
- full responsive implementation;
- speculative component-system expansion;
- a separate Engineering Record index;
- a separate Engineering Approach top-level destination;
- complete portfolio content for every future section;
- final production interactions;
- treating prototype operation as implementation verification;
- redesigning accepted Stage 05 Portfolio Orientation without new evidence.

Stage 06 must not silently expand into production implementation or a complete portfolio build.

---

## 4. Expected Outcome

At the end of Stage 06, the project should have:

> **A traversable prototype that allows a visitor to move from Portfolio Orientation into Project 01, understand the Engineering Approach and Engineering Record, and proceed into Deep Engineering with sufficient contextual continuity to evaluate the intended experience.**

The engineering record should also provide sufficient evidence to understand:

- what was materialized;
- what interactions were introduced;
- what was deliberately not introduced;
- what was learned through experiencing the prototype;
- what was revised;
- what remains uncertain or deferred.

---

## 5. Acceptance

Stage 06 acceptance will be based on evidence that:

### Experience

- Portfolio Orientation provides a clear entry into Project 01.
- Project 01 is understandable without requiring immediate technical depth.
- Engineering Approach and Engineering Record are discoverable within the Project 01 experience.
- Deep Engineering can be reached as a deliberate increase in inspection depth.
- The progressive-depth model remains coherent.

### Interaction

- Intended prototype transitions work.
- No important transition depends on an invented destination.
- Interaction behavior supports the intended experience rather than merely demonstrating tool capability.

### Evidence

- Significant prototype decisions are recorded.
- Meaningful problems discovered during experience are recorded.
- Revisions are traceable.
- Unresolved matters remain explicitly unresolved.

### Boundary

- Stage 06 has not silently expanded into production implementation.
- No Stage 05 structural decision has been reopened without evidence.

Prototype completion is not implementation verification or product acceptance. Stage 06 acceptance establishes that the intended experience has been sufficiently materialized and inspected to support the next stage.

---

## 6. Authorization

The approved authorization is:

> **Controlled materialization, connection, inspection, and evidence-led revision of the Stage 06 prototype within the approved Purpose, Scope, Non-Scope, Expected Outcome, and Acceptance criteria.**

This authorization permits execution of:

- prototype structure and connections;
- the Project 01 experience;
- Engineering Approach and Engineering Record exposure;
- the path into Deep Engineering;
- necessary prototype interactions;
- prototype inspection;
- evidence-led revisions;
- documentation of decisions and findings.

This authorization does not permit:

- production implementation;
- frontend coding;
- backend or system development;
- redesigning accepted Stage 05 structure without new evidence;
- inventing destinations or content;
- creating a separate Engineering Record index;
- treating prototype completion as implementation verification;
- declaring Stage 06 accepted before its acceptance criteria have been verified.

If evidence indicates that the approved boundary must materially change, execution stops at that decision boundary and the proposed change must be reviewed and authorized separately.

---

## 7. Control Sequence

Stage 06 follows:

**Approved Boundary → Authorized Execution → Evidence → Verification → Acceptance**

The authorization to execute within this boundary does not constitute acceptance of the resulting prototype.

---

## 8. Decision Provenance

The Stage 06 boundary was established through the project's engineering control sequence:

**Observation → Interpretation → Proposal → Human Decision → Authorization → Execution → Verification**

The Purpose, Scope, Non-Scope, Expected Outcome, and Acceptance elements were approved by the human project authority.

The execution boundary and control against future scope expansion were subsequently approved and authorized by the human project authority.

No agent proposal is treated as a human decision or authorization.
